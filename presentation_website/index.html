The Critical Problem

Why Navigation Collapses in Tunnels

When satellite line-of-sight is lost, conventional navigation apps either stop completely or drift off course. Standard smartphones lack wheel speed sensors, while pure mathematical acceleration integration explodes exponentially.

Standard Navigation Apps

Signal Loss & Screen Freeze

In tunnels, underpasses, and high-rise corridors, GNSS satellite fixes drop to zero. Consumer mapping apps freeze, report "Searching for GPS", or snap wildly to elevated flyovers or adjacent service roads, causing missed exits and dangerous driver distraction.

TUNNEL OUTAGE: 0 SATELLITES VISIBLE
Classical Inertial (INS)

Quadratic Drift Explosion

Open-loop double integration of smartphone accelerometers causes minute sensor biases to compound quadratically ($\Delta p \propto \frac{1}{2} b_a t^2$), while gyro bias couples with gravity cubically. Within 30 seconds of an outage, position drifts by >140 meters off-road.

30-SEC DRIFT: >140 METERS (>180% ERROR)

Technical Approach

The 3 Engineering Pillars of IDR

How our software pipeline replaces open-loop double integration with a tightly coupled Neuro-Kalman state estimator.

PILLAR 01 • NEURAL KINEMATICS

Causal SE-TCN Speed Estimator

An 8-channel Temporal Convolutional Network with Squeeze-and-Excitation attention infers vehicle forward speed directly from chassis and transmission vibration harmonics, bypassing accelerometer integration errors.

$$\hat{v}_k, \sigma_k^2 = f_{\text{SE-TCN}}(\mathbf{W}_{\text{IMU}}^{k-39:k})$$
  • Predicts heteroscedastic uncertainty ($\sigma^2$) to adaptively tune Kalman gain
  • 11.2ms inference latency on Snapdragon 7 Gen 3 (10 Hz update loop)
PILLAR 02 • STATE ESTIMATION

9D Extended Kalman Filter (NHC)

A 9-state EKF tracks position, velocity, and orientation while strictly enforcing physical Non-Holonomic Constraints: wheeled road vehicles cannot slide sideways or jump vertically through asphalt ($v_y = 0, v_z = 0$).

$$v_y^b \approx 0, \quad v_z^b \approx 0 \quad (\text{Zero Lateral Skid})$$
  • Prevents heading divergence by continuously anchoring lateral velocity to zero
  • Zero Velocity Update (ZUPT): Locks position at red lights and traffic jams
PILLAR 03 • INDIAN ROAD RESILIENCY

Pothole Gating & Map Snapping

Tailored for Indian driving conditions. Jerk variance gating temporarily freezes vertical acceleration integration during pothole and speed breaker impacts, while offline vector map matching keeps trajectory on the road network.

$$\operatorname{Var}(\dot{a}_z) > \tau_{\text{jerk}} \implies \text{Freeze Accel Integration}$$
  • Jerk Variance Gate ($\tau = 450\text{ m}^2/\text{s}^6$) neutralizes road shock spikes
  • 100% offline 3D vector map matching operates without internet connection

SLIDE 2 • PROPOSED SOLUTION

3-Pillar Solution Architecture

Replacing open-loop double-integration with neural velocity regression and physical constraint filtering.

Pillar 1: Neural Kinematics

8-Channel Causal SE-TCN extracts forward vehicle speed directly from chassis vibration harmonics, predicting both velocity and uncertainty.

Pillar 2: 9D EKF with NHC

Enforces physical constraints (cars do not slip laterally or bounce through pavement), eliminating heading divergence.

Pillar 3: Road Resiliency

Jerk variance gating neutralizes pothole shock spikes, while offline 3D vector map matching snaps trajectory to drivable roads.

Presenter Script: "Rather than integrating noisy accelerometers twice, our neural network infers forward velocity directly from vibration harmonics at 10 Hz with 11ms latency."
SLIDE 3 • TECHNICAL PIPELINE

Dual-Tier Implementation Pipeline

Real-time edge execution across high-level Flutter UI and low-level C++20 SIMD core.

100 Hz Sensor Engine

Triaxial accelerometer and gyroscope sampled at 100 Hz, downsampled to 10 Hz with sliding 4.0-second window.

ONNX Mobile Runtime

Dilated causal convolutions executed locally via ONNX Runtime Mobile, consuming <4% CPU on modern devices.

Offline Vector Engine

Offline OpenStreetMap vector topology loaded locally into memory, requiring zero cellular data during tunnel outages.

Presenter Script: "Our implementation features a dual-tier stack: a production Flutter Android application and an ultra-lightweight 933 KB pure C++20 SIMD NativeActivity engine."
SLIDE 4 • FEASIBILITY & ROAD RISKS

Engineered for Indian Road Conditions

Mitigating road anomalies: potholes, speed breakers, stop-and-go queues, and multi-tier flyovers.

Pothole Jerk Gating

Monitors vertical jerk derivative. If jerk exceeds 18 m/s³, acceleration integration freezes to prevent phantom speed surges.

Standstill ZUPT Lock

Zero Velocity Update detects vehicle idling at red lights and locks speed to exact 0.00 km/h, preventing sensor crawl.

Zero-Jump Handoff

When exiting tunnels, smooth covariance shrinking re-converges to GNSS fixes without jarring screen teleports.

Presenter Script: "Indian roads have severe pavement irregularities. Our jerk variance filter discriminates between true vehicular forward acceleration and vertical shock from potholes."
