The Challenge

Why Navigation Breaks in Blackout Zones

When satellite line-of-sight is blocked, conventional navigation systems either freeze entirely or drift catastrophically off-road.

Consumer Apps

Signal Drop & Screen Freeze

Inside tunnels, underpasses, and urban canyons, GNSS satellite fixes drop to zero. Consumer apps freeze, report "Searching for GPS", or jump erratically to nearby flyovers and service roads, causing missed exits and dangerous driver confusion.

Classical Inertial (INS)

Quadratic Drift Explosion

Standard dead reckoning relies on double-integrating noisy accelerometer data. Sensor biases compound quadratically (Δp ∝ ½ b t²), while gyro bias tilts gravity into the acceleration axis, causing drift to exceed 140 meters in just 30 seconds.

30-SEC DRIFT: >140 METERS OFF-ROAD
IDR-Navigator

The 5-Stage Hybrid Cascade

Instead of raw acceleration integration, IDR-Navigator infers instantaneous forward velocity using a causal SE-TCN, fuses it with an Extended Kalman Filter enforcing vehicle physics constraints, and locks the trajectory to verified road geometries.

MEAN DRIFT: 3.47% • ₹0 HARDWARE RETROFIT
Architecture

The 5-Stage Hybrid Pipeline

How our software pipeline replaces open-loop integration with a robust, physics-constrained state estimator.

STAGE 01

Attitude Observer

Dynamic Mahony SO(3) filter continuously decouples gravity and phone tilt from vehicle acceleration vectors.

STAGE 02

Vibration Gate

Savitzky-Golay jerk filter isolates chassis vibration harmonics and speed-breaker shocks from genuine motion.

STAGE 03

Neural Speed (TCN)

8-channel Causal Dilated SE-TCN predicts instantaneous forward speed at 20 Hz without satellite fixes.

STAGE 04

Zero-Velocity (ZUPT)

Detects full vehicle stops at traffic lights instantly, freezing coordinate drift without relying on GPS Doppler.

STAGE 05

Vector Map Snapping

Projects state estimates onto offline OpenStreetMap road polylines, clamping cross-track error to the roadway.
