Experimental Protocol & Trial Regimes

TRIAL 01 · SEGMENT 00:00 – 03:31

Highway Underpass Tunnel Outage (NH60)

Conducted along the Pune–Nashik Highway (NH60). Location services are deliberately toggled OFF at 00:07 via the Android notification shade to replicate instantaneous GNSS carrier loss upon entering subterranean highway bores. The 8-channel causal SE-TCN speed estimator and 3D vector road snapper maintain continuous lane lock without satellite fixes, successfully isolating road pothole shocks through Savitzky-Golay jerk gating.

TRIAL 02 · SEGMENT 03:31 – 05:59

Sangamner Bypass Extended Blackout

An extended multi-kilometer blackout trial conducted over sweeping highway curves. Evaluates thermal stability, on-device NPU latency (<2.2 ms/step), and single-track vehicle dynamics. Demonstrates that our Mahony SO(3) attitude observer decouples roll banking during cornering, while Stage 5 OSM map snapping bounds cross-track lateral error across more than 8,000 meters traversed.
