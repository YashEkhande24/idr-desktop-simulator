What This Field Demonstration Demonstrates

Phase 1 • 00:00 – 00:15

Open-Sky Baseline Calibration

Vehicle travels on open highway with healthy satellite coverage. The system calibrates phone cabin mounting attitude and initializes the 9D Extended Kalman Filter state against GNSS Doppler velocity.

Phase 2 • 00:15 – 00:45

Tunnel Entry & GPS Denial

Satellite signal drops completely to 0 satellites. Classical double-integration quickly diverges into tunnel walls (>140m error). IDR-Navigator switches to on-device neural speed estimation and locks to the driving lane.

Phase 3 • 00:45 – 01:14

Potholes, Stops & Re-Convergence

Demonstrates pothole jerk rejection without false acceleration spikes, zero-velocity updates (ZUPT) at standstill, and seamless re-convergence upon tunnel exit without trajectory jumping.
