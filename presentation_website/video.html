What This Field Demonstration Demonstrates

Phase 1 • 00:00 – 00:07

Open-Sky Baseline Calibration

Vehicle begins under clear open skies with healthy satellite lock. The green GNSS ACTIVE indicator verifies healthy satellite signal with ±0.7m accuracy, establishing baseline Doppler alignment.

Phase 2 • 00:07 – 00:10

Manual GNSS Denial Trigger

Quick settings shade is pulled down on camera to sever GNSS location, simulating entering a complete blackout tunnel (0 satellites). Red outage telemetry banner slides in immediately.

Phase 3 • 00:10 – 03:31

Underpass Tunnel Outage (Trial 1)

Pune-Nashik Highway (NH60) tunnel outage run. 8-Ch Dilated SE-TCN speed estimator and 3D vector road snapping maintain lane lock without satellite signal, rejecting road bumps and stops.

Phase 4 • 03:31 – 05:59

Extended Blackout Trial (Trial 2)

Sangamner Bypass extended satellite denial run demonstrating standstill ZUPT and vibration filter resilience over prolonged outages without coordinate drift or jumping.
