Real-World Field Demonstration

Synchronized Dual-Feed HUD Trial

Continuous vehicular dead reckoning evaluated over highway underpasses and extended bypass runs during complete GNSS denial.

Trial 01: Highway Underpass Tunnel Outage

Pune–Nashik Highway (NH60). Location deliberately toggled OFF at 00:07 to test instant satellite denial. Neural speed estimator and road clamping hold vehicle in-lane throughout the outage.

00:00 - Open Sky 00:07 - GPS Cut 00:35 - Traversal

Trial 02: Sangamner Bypass Extended Blackout

High-speed bypass curves tested over an extended multi-minute blackout. Demonstrates edge NPU efficiency, standstill ZUPT, and vibration rejection on a single-track vehicle.

03:31 - Bypass Start 03:50 - Curve Banking 05:15 - 8km Stability

Trial Breakdown & Engineering Phases

Phase 1 • 00:00 – 00:07

Open-Sky Baseline Calibration

Vehicle begins under clear open skies with healthy satellite lock. The green GNSS ACTIVE indicator verifies healthy satellite signal with ±0.7m accuracy, establishing baseline Doppler alignment.

Phase 2 • 00:07 – 00:14

Manual GNSS Denial Trigger

The quick settings shade is pulled down on camera to sever GNSS location, simulating entering a complete blackout tunnel (0 satellites). Red outage telemetry banner slides in immediately.

Phase 3 • 00:14 – 03:31

Underpass Traversal & Clamping

8-channel Causal SE-TCN speed estimator and 3D vector road snapping maintain lane lock without satellite signal, rejecting road bumps, uneven asphalt, and temporary deceleration stops.

Phase 4 • 03:31 – 05:59

Extended Bypass Blackout Run

Sangamner Bypass extended satellite denial run demonstrating standstill ZUPT and vibration filter resilience over prolonged outages without coordinate drift or teleportation.
